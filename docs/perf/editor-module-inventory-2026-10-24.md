## What the three named families are worth

The loader (`src/js/route-deferred-loader.js`) fetches the deferred chunk on `requestIdleCallback` (2 s timeout) or the first pointer/key/touch event, so a move does not reduce **transferred** bytes — it moves parse/eval and the module's own load-time work off the critical path. For the three candidates the parse/eval is small (3–11 KB gzip each, ~34 KB for all three including the typography trio that cannot move); the real saving would be the load-time DOM binding in `social-card.js`/`storyboard.js` and the eager consumer fix in `ai-assistant-pro.js`.

**Measured finding, not a promise:** all three named families have an eager consumer or a load-time DOM binding, so this pass moved none of them. Doing it properly needs the consumer rewrite first (AI) or a lazy-mount change (social/storyboard), each of which is its own behavioural change and must land with a probe of the feature being opened cold. That is the next step, and it is why this item stops at measurement rather than shipping a deferral that can leave a button dead for the first two seconds.
