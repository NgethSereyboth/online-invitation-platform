Privacy

This platform is designed so invitations can operate without collecting unnecessary guest information. The invitation owner controls whether optional analytics and external media are enabled.

Invitation visits

When an invitation requires analytics consent, a visit is not added to its view statistics until the guest chooses to allow analytics. The platform records aggregate visit events rather than advertising profiles.

Guest information

RSVP is optional. When enabled, the host may collect information needed for the event, such as attendance, guest count, meal, transport or accommodation preferences. Guest wishes are independently optional. Invitation owners should request only information they genuinely need.

External media

YouTube and SoundCloud are external providers. When external-media consent is required, those providers are not loaded until the guest allows them. Provider privacy terms then apply to the embedded content.

Personalized invitation links

Personal guest links may contain a short-lived or revocable credential. They should be treated like private invitation links. General sharing controls remove personalized and private-access parameters.

Account controls

Account holders can review active sessions, export their data, configure privacy preferences, and schedule account deletion. Deleted invitations use a recovery period before permanent purge where configured.

Storage and retention

Uploaded files can be stored locally or through a configured private object-storage provider. Production operators are responsible for configuring encryption, lifecycle policies, backups, legal retention requirements and their own public contact information.

This project provides technical privacy controls. A production operator should replace or supplement this general notice with the legally appropriate policy for the jurisdiction, organization and services being used.
